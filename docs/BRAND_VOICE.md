# Hunter3D — Brand Voice

## Brand personality

Hunter3D should sound:

* welcoming
* clear
* practical
* creative
* trustworthy
* professional
* approachable

The brand should communicate care and attention without sounding overly clinical.

It should communicate creativity without sounding childish.

## Audience

The website may be read by:

* occupational therapists
* therapy professionals
* educators
* parents and caregivers
* people searching for sensory or activity resources
* customers interested in other 3D printed products

Write for people, not specialists.

Technical terminology may be used when it is useful and understandable in context.

## Writing principles

Prefer:

* natural Brazilian Portuguese
* concise sentences
* clear product explanations
* practical information
* concrete descriptions
* friendly calls to action
* easy scanning

Avoid:

* exaggerated marketing
* artificial enthusiasm
* corporate jargon
* generic AI language
* unnecessarily technical language
* excessive use of adjectives
* infantilized language

## Product descriptions

Describe what the product is and how it may be used.

Prioritize useful information such as:

* purpose
* characteristics
* format
* dimensions when relevant
* material
* suggested activities
* customization when available

Do not invent therapeutic benefits.

Avoid claims such as:

* "trata..."
* "cura..."
* "garante..."
* "melhora o desenvolvimento..."
* "comprovadamente melhora..."
* "ideal para autismo..."

unless such wording has been explicitly supplied and appropriately supported.

Prefer wording such as:

* "pode ser utilizado em atividades de..."
* "recurso pensado para..."
* "pode auxiliar na organização de atividades..."
* "indicado para uso em..."
* "permite diferentes possibilidades de uso..."

when factually appropriate.

## Conversion

The primary objective is not an online purchase.

The desired action is starting a conversation.

Calls to action should therefore sound natural.

Prefer:

* Tenho interesse
* Quero saber mais
* Falar sobre este produto
* Chamar no WhatsApp
* Ver detalhes

Avoid:

* Comprar agora
* Finalizar compra
* Adicionar ao carrinho

unless the business model changes.

Client decision: Hunter3D has an approved exception for a client-side
"sacola" feature (see CLIENT_BRIEF.md's "Approved exception" section), so
"Adicionar à sacola" is the one sanctioned CTA of this kind on this project.
It only queues products for a single WhatsApp message — it does not imply
checkout or online payment. The WhatsApp-based CTAs above remain primary.

## Tone

The website should feel like a real person or small business presenting carefully made products.

It should not sound like:

* a large corporation
* a hospital
* a generic e-commerce marketplace
* an AI-generated marketing page

## Final test

Before considering any text finished, ask:

"Would a real seller naturally say this to a customer?"

If not, rewrite it.
