Perform a complete review of the current website.

Follow CLAUDE.md.

Use the appropriate specialist agents:

- web-designer for UI/UX
- seo-reviewer for SEO
- qa-reviewer for technical QA

Combine their findings.

Prioritize findings as:

1. Blocking
2. Important
3. Nice to have

Do not make unnecessary changes only for stylistic preference.

If blocking issues exist, fix them when appropriate and rerun the relevant validation.

Finish with a production-readiness verdict.
